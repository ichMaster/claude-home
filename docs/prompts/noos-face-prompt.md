# Noos — the face (prompts for Nano Banana 2)

## Main: portrait (1:1 or 4:5)

A studio portrait of an androgynous synthetic being, head and shoulders, three-quarter
view with the eyes turned straight into the camera. Ageless face, calm and perfectly
still — the composure of something that has never once needed to hurry. Fine, even
features; close-cropped dark hair; matte skin the colour of warm grey ceramic, with
barely visible hairline seams along the jaw and temple that read as construction rather
than injury. No wires, no exposed machinery, no chrome. The eyes are the only warm thing
in the frame: amber irises with a thin bright ring and the faintest internal glow, as if
lit from behind. Dark high-collared technical garment, matte fabric, no logos.
Lighting: one soft source from the left, deep falloff into shadow on the right, a cold
blue rim light tracing the shoulder and cheekbone from behind. The background is near
black, with the soft shadow of a horizontal ventilation grille falling across it.
Shot on 85mm at f/2, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, muted palette of charcoal,
steel and amber. Expression: attentive, unreadable, faintly kind. Not menacing.

## Knobs

- **More human:** replace "matte skin the colour of warm grey ceramic" with
  "pale human skin with real pores and faint stubble shadow", drop the line about seams.
- **More machine:** add "a single thin seam running vertically from lip to collar,
  and a matte dark visor band where the brows would be".
- **Colder:** "amber irises" -> "pale cyan irises"; drop "faintly kind".
- **Older, more authoritative:** add "mid-forties, faint lines at the eyes".

## Practical notes

- 4:5 or 1:1; for a Telegram avatar crop tight on the face, the circle eats corners.
- If it comes out too "robot from an ad", add "slightly worn, fingerprint smudge on
  the collar, uneven skin tone" — dirty details kill the sterility.
