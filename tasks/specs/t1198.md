# t1198 — astro_learn add validates --period and requires --day for month and year

## Problem / goal

`astro_learn.py add --period` accepts any string, and `--day` defaults to today. Two silent
failures follow (nightly review 2026-11-17, Medium):

1. A typo or a Russian spelling (`месяц`, `мiсяць` with a Latin і) stores a record that
   `covering()` — which matches the exact strings `місяць`/`рік` — will never return. The
   month horizon just never appears, with no warning at ingest.
2. `covering()` matches a monthly record by the `(year, month)` of its stored day. The
   astrologer publishes December's month on 25 November; `add --period місяць` without
   `--day` stores it under 2026-11-25, so the December text feeds the rest of November's
   mornings. The first-of-month convention exists only in a docstring.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** `add --period месяц` (an unknown value) is refused by the CLI
  (argparse `choices`), nothing is stored.
- **TC2 — regression:** `add --period місяць` without `--day` is refused with a message
  naming the flag — a month forecast must say which month it is for, today is a guess.
- **TC3 —** `add --period місяць --day 2026-12-01` stores the record and `covering()` finds
  it for a December day and not for a November one; `--period день` without `--day` keeps
  working as before.

## Approach

`choices=("день", "тиждень", "місяць", "рік")` on `--period`, and an explicit refusal in the
`add` branch when the period is `місяць`/`рік` and `--day` was not given. No normalisation of
the stored day: `covering()` matches any day inside the month/year, so forcing the first adds
nothing once the day is explicit. The panel always passes `--period день` with an explicit
`--day`, so nothing automated changes behaviour.
