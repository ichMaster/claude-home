# t1126 — the forecast becomes checkable claims with time windows

The owner, 2026-11-01: «те, що виходить, зовсім не працює і не дає ні прогнозу, ні
читабельно». He approved the new shape by ear the same evening («звучить добре тепер»).

## Problem

Two failures, both of them mine.

**It is not a forecast.** The reading describes the day's air — «напруга в комунікації»,
«день вимагає обережності». Nothing in it can be checked in the evening or used in the
morning, so the calibration loop had nothing to measure and the 96 lines of rules it
accumulated have no score.

**It does not read.** It was built for an eye: five blocks with headings, 3300 characters,
about four minutes of audio. He listens — one stream, where a heading means nothing and
1800 characters of «ІСТОРІЯ» in the middle is where attention goes.

## The new shape, as approved

Voice first, 60–90 seconds, ~1200 characters, four parts and no spoken headings:

1. **ДЕНЬ** — one sentence: what kind of day this is. No planets.
2. **ПРОГНОЗ** — two or three claims, each with a **time window** and a **recognisable
   sign**: when, what, and how it will look if it happened.
3. **ДІЯ** — one line: what to do or not do today.
4. **ДУГА** — one sentence: what holds for weeks, and whether this is its peak.

A planet is named only where it explains a claim, in half a sentence. No degrees, no dates,
no house glosses in the spoken copy. The reference — **ОСНОВА** — is text only, and the long
material stays in the log and on the Zirka screen.

## The pipeline shrinks from seventeen calls to three

`facts` (local, unchanged) → **pick** → **write** → **check**, plus the biorhythms call.

- **pick** (`status/mood/pick.md`): given every computed fact with its window, the day's Moon
  events **with their clock times**, the retrogrades, yesterday's forecast and the
  calibration rules — choose the arc, two or three candidate claims each tied to a real
  window, and the action. The windows come from the ephemeris, so timing is arithmetic and
  not invented.
- **write** (`status/mood/day.md`): the four parts, inside the budgets.
- **check** (`status/mood/check.md`): every claim has a window and a sign; no degree, date or
  house gloss in the spoken part; the arc matches the computed duration; nothing contradicts
  yesterday without saying so. Returns the corrected text, so no fourth call.

## Claims become data

Each claim is stored in `status/state/forecast-claims.jsonl` with the day, an id, its window,
its text and its sign. The evening question asks about **exactly those** instead of
re-deriving something to ask, and `mood_feedback.py score` reports the hit rate over the last
`astro.score_days` days. That is what gives the rules a score.

**The question is asked in the forecast's own style** (the owner, 2026-11-01: «десь в такому
стилі треба б було питати про день, який пройшов»): one short yes/no per claim, naming the
window and the sign it was promised by — «до обіду тебе перепитали або зрозуміли навпаки?» —
and nothing else. No «як пройшов день», no astrology in the question, and no question at all
about a claim that was never made.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** the new blocks parse out of a model answer; a missing block is empty, not fatal.
- **TC2 —** `claims(text)` returns one entry per ПРОГНОЗ line with its window and sign; a line
  without a window or without a sign is **dropped and counted**, never sent as a claim.
- **TC3 —** the spoken copy contains no degree figure, no `dd.mm` date and no ОСНОВА block.
- **TC4 —** a reading over budget is compressed; one inside it is untouched.
- **TC5 —** claims are appended with the day and stable ids; two runs on one day replace that
  day's claims rather than duplicating them.
- **TC6 —** the evening question is built from the day's stored claims: one yes/no per
  claim, each naming its window and its sign, in the forecast's own words; no claims means no
  question rather than an invented one; and the question text contains no planet name.
- **TC7 —** `score` counts hits and misses over the window and reports «немає даних» when
  nothing was answered.
- **TC8 —** the message assembles in the new order, and the voice copy drops the header line.
