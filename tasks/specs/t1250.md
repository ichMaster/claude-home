# t1250 — a midnight-crossing window is labeled by its portion inside the day

## Problem / goal

t1229 made `part_of_day("23:10", "03:12")` return «вночі» (192 of 242 minutes fall
after midnight). But in day D's reading «вночі» is 00:00–05:00 **of day D itself**:
`PARTS` maps 0–5 h to it and `PART_ORDER` puts the «вночі» line first in ПРОГНОЗ.
The astrologer anchors a midnight-crossing window to its start day, so his page for
D carries «с 23:10 до 03:12» meaning the night *after* D's evening — and the 08:15
reading for D presented it as the already-passed night at the day's opening,
indistinguishable from a genuine small-hours window. The state was narrated about a
day early and in the wrong slot. The pre-t1229 «ввечері» was the right label; t1229
traded a collapse-to-one-minute bug for a day-anchoring one. (Nightly review
2026-11-23, Medium, verified against live reference data — ~10% of his timed
windows cross midnight.)

## Test cases

- **TC1 (regression) —** `part_of_day("23:10", "03:12") == "ввечері"` and
  `part_of_day("22:22", "03:09") == "ввечері"` — the portion inside the day decides.
  Fails before the change (t1229 returned «вночі»).
- **TC2 —** t1229's actual point still holds: no collapse to one minute —
  `("19:00", "00:30")` is «ввечері» by 5 hours of evening, not by a single minute;
  a genuine small-hours window `("01:00", "03:00")` stays «вночі»; `("23:00",
  "23:50")` without a wrap is untouched.

## Approach

When `end < start`, clip the window to midnight (`end = 24*60`) instead of running
it into the next day's parts. Only the portion of the window inside this day
exists for this day's reading; the small hours belong to the next day's reading.
The second-day scan loop becomes dead and is removed.
