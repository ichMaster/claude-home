# t1143 — astro v2, phase 1: read his finished calendar

## Problem / goal

Phase 1 of the plan in `docs/astro-v2-reference.md`. The owner supplies a working astrologer's
hourly Moon calendar once a week; this phase turns the day's page of it into the morning
reading, with no astrology of our own. The sky comes from him, the delivery is ours.

Why first: it is the only one of the three phases that can be measured mechanically rather
than judged (did every loud window survive, is every sentence traceable), and it produces
something listenable on day one.

Depends on t1140's scaffold — `status/mood2.py`, `status/mood2/`, the `astro2` config block,
its own log and state, no timer. Build that first if it is still missing.

## What a run does

`status/mood2.py --day <date>` (default today), `--send` to actually deliver, nothing on a
timer until the owner says v2 replaces v1.

1. **Load** his entry for that date from `status/state/astro-reference.jsonl` (the existing
   store; intake is already built — `astro_learn.py add --split`, the Zirka chat's
   `reference` classification, and the inbox).
2. **Read** (`status/mood2/read.md`) — one call turning his page into structured items, each
   with an id: kind (`moon_sign`, `moon_house`, `void`, `ingress`, `window`, `natal`), the
   clock window, the intensity 0-5 (his `✤` count), the bodies and the printed formula, and
   his meaning. A model call rather than a pattern: his layout varies, and t1033 already
   established that the headers are read, not matched.
3. **Select** (`status/mood2/pick.md`) — what the day is about, at most `astro2.items_max`
   items. Mandatory: every item at or above `astro2.loud_min` intensity (3) and every `natal`
   item. Then the Moon's sign and house as the day's frame, and the void window when it falls
   in waking hours.
4. **Write** (`status/mood2/day.md`) — Ukrainian, for the ear, inside `astro2.budget_chars`;
   windows in words, clock times allowed because they are what makes a window usable;
   personal transits said separately from the general Moon weather. Each written line returns
   the ids it came from.
5. **Validate** — deterministic, no model: every id a line claims exists in step 2's items,
   every mandatory item appears, the spoken copy carries no Latin letter and no figures except
   clock times. A reading that fails validation is not sent; the failure is logged and said
   plainly.

## Test cases

A synthetic fixture stands in for his text (`tests/data/reference-day.txt`, invented figures
in his layout — his real calendar is never committed). Model calls are stubbed; nothing
reaches the network, Telegram or the live state directory.

- **TC1 —** given the fixture day in the store, `read` produces items with kind, window,
  intensity and formula, and `pick` keeps **every** item of intensity ≥ 3 and **every**
  `natal` item, even when that exceeds the preferred count.
- **TC2 —** his text for the date is missing: the run sends nothing, says so distinctly, and
  does not call `status/astro.py` at all — phase 1 never fills a gap with a computed day.
- **TC3 —** a written line claiming a source id that does not exist in the read items is
  rejected by validation and nothing is sent (this is the invention guard).
- **TC4 —** a mandatory item (intensity 5, or a `natal` line) missing from the written
  reading is rejected by validation.
- **TC5 —** the spoken copy contains no Latin letter, and no digit outside a clock time;
  length is within `astro2.budget_chars`.
- **TC6 —** the output is Ukrainian, not his Russian: it contains none of `ы ъ э ё`.
- **TC7 —** v2 writes only to its own paths: after a run, every v1 state file
  (`status/state/mood.json`, `status/state/mood.log`, `status/state/forecast-claims.jsonl`)
  is byte-identical.
- **TC8 — edge:** a day with nothing loud (no item above intensity 2, no `natal` line) still
  produces a reading, built from the Moon's sign, house and void window, rather than an empty
  or apologetic message.
- **TC9 — edge:** with no `astro2` block in config the run refuses outright (inherited from
  t1140 TC5) — a silent fallback would let v2 write into v1's history.

## Approach

The pipeline above: three model calls and one deterministic validator. Traceability is carried
in the data — the writing stage returns `{line, sources: [ids]}` — so the invention guard is
arithmetic rather than another model's opinion.

Sending stays manual (`--send`) through the forecast's own bot and voice, so the two readings
can be heard side by side on the same morning before anything is switched.

Deliberately out of scope: our own interpretation of anything (phase 2, t1144), computing the
factor list ourselves (phase 3, t1145), the ingress and void-of-course solvers (also phase 3 —
in phase 1 both come from his text), touching v1, and any timer change.
