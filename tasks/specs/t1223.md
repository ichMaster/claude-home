# t1223 — a weekly reference splits into daily records or is refused

## Problem / goal

`astro_learn.py add --period тиждень` is accepted (status/astro_learn.py:272-273) but the
record is readable by nothing: `for_day()` keeps only `period == "день"`
(astro_learn.py:156-158) and `covering()` matches only `місяць`/`рік` (:180-183). A weekly
forward ingested the documented way — zirka_intent classifies it «тиждень», the session runs
`add --split --period тиждень` — prints «записано…» per day, yet every piece is dead data:
`mood2.py --send` at 08:15 raises NoCalendar («еталонного календаря … немає») for dates the
astrologer actually covered. The t1198 principle — anything the readers cannot find is
refused at the door — was applied to unknown periods and to undated month/year forwards, but
«тиждень» stayed in the choices with no consumer and no refusal. Two finders reported this
independently (also at :157).

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** given `add --split --period тиждень` over a multi-day text, when the records
  are stored, then each piece carries `period: "день"` and `for_day()` finds its day
  (regression — fails before the change).
- **TC2 —** given `add --period тиждень` without `--split`, then the command refuses with
  a message naming `--split`, and nothing is stored.
- **TC3 — edge:** `день`, `місяць`, `рік` behave as before.

## Approach

In the `add` branch of `main()`: `тиждень` without `--split` exits with guidance; with
`--split`, the pieces are stored as `день` (each piece is one day's calendar — that is what
the split produces). `for_day()`/`covering()` stay untouched.
