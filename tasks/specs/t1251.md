# t1251 — a failed wide stage degrades to no background

## Problem / goal

In `status/mood2.py` `run()`, the wide stage calls
`wide_relevant(wide_entries(day, note), day)` with no local handling. On a cache
miss, `wide_entries` calls the model: a timeout or non-zero exit raises
`StageFailed`, an answer with no JSON bracket raises `Unfaithful`, and both
propagate out of `run()` before the day stage — exit 3, the whole 08:15 reading
lost — even though the daily calendar was present and readable. Four lines below,
an empty-but-valid `[]` from the same stage is deliberately tolerated as a flake
(t1197), so the module's own contract says the stage is supplementary. One chatty
«записів немає» reply from the wide model costs the owner the entire morning.
(Nightly review 2026-11-23, Medium, verified.)

## Test cases

- **TC1 (regression) —** given `wide_entries` raising `StageFailed`, when `run()`
  executes with otherwise good stages, then the reading completes (the day stage
  output is produced) and the failure is noted («ширші горизонти…»). Fails before
  the change (`StageFailed` propagates).
- **TC2 —** the same for `Unfaithful` from a bracket-less wide answer.
- **TC3 —** a working wide stage still contributes its day positions (existing
  tests in `test_mood2_wide.py`).

## Approach

Wrap the wide stage body in `try/except (StageFailed, Unfaithful)`: note the loss
and continue with the empty shape `{"day": [], "period": [], "dropped": []}` —
the same degradation as an empty extraction. Failures of the read, pick and day
stages still stop the run: they are the reading.
