# t1192 — the heavy positions of a day are read as heavy

## Problem / goal

The owner, 16 November, 04:18 and corrected at 04:21: «врахуй що максимум ✤ може бути 4 і
якщо їх багато то ставити на цьому акцент в прогнозі і більше даталей в самарі» →
«перепрошую максимум це 5».

So the scale is **0 to 5**, which is what `read.md` and `docs/astro-v2-reference.md` already
say and what the thirteen stored reference days contain: runs of 1 (32), 2 (3), 3 (4),
4 (1) and 5 (3). Nothing to correct there.

What is missing is the second half: **a heavy day must sound heavy.** `day.md` carries one
soft line about it («Голосне звучить голосно») and `{{НАГОЛОС}}` — which `run()` fills with
*every* chosen id, so the instruction «позиції X — найважчі в добі, вони мають займати більше
місця, ніж решта» names the whole day and therefore says nothing. Nothing computes how heavy
the day is, and nothing checks the result, so a window he marked `✤✤✤✤✤` can stand in one row
with a `✤` trifle and `ДЕНЬ` — what he calls «самарі» — can describe the day's mood without
ever naming what presses on it.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** a day whose loudest window is weight 5, with a reading whose `ДЕНЬ`
  marks only a quiet position → `check_reading` reports that `ДЕНЬ` does not name the
  heaviest. The same reading with the loud window's id in `ДЕНЬ` passes.
- **TC2 —** a day whose loudest window is weight 2 → no such complaint, whatever `ДЕНЬ` marks:
  a calm day is not forced to shout.
- **TC3 —** `pressure(items)` reports the day's top weight, the loud blocks (≥ `loud_min`, 3)
  and the accented blocks (≥ `accent_min`, 4); a `natal` line inherits its window's block, so
  one window with three natal hits counts once, and marking the natal hit in `ДЕНЬ` names its
  window.
- **TC4 —** three or more loud blocks make the day heavy even with no single window at the top
  (`heavy_blocks`), and `pressure_text()` says so in Ukrainian — in digits, with no `✤` in it,
  because a mark echoed into the reading costs a retry.
- **TC5 —** five marks are the top of the scale and accented, not an error.
- **TC6 — edge:** a day with no weighted position at all yields no accented blocks and a
  pressure line that says the day is level; `check_reading` demands nothing of `ДЕНЬ`.
- **TC7 —** the writing stage is handed `pressure_text()` and the accented ids, not the whole
  selection.

## Approach

- `pressure(items)` in `status/mood2.py` — arithmetic over the blocks `blocks()` already
  builds: top weight, loud block ids, accented block ids. `is_heavy()` and `pressure_text()`
  on top of it, so no model has to count marks.
- `{{НАГОЛОС}}` stops meaning «everything chosen» and starts meaning the accented blocks (the
  loud ones when nothing reaches 4); `{{ТИСК}}` carries the pressure line. `day.md` gains the
  rule that goes with them: on a heavy day the accented window's `ПРОГНОЗ` line is the longest
  of them, and `ДЕНЬ` is two sentences naming what presses rather than one vague one.
- `check_reading` hard-checks only the part that is cheap to satisfy: on an accented day
  `ДЕНЬ` must mark a position from an accented block. The line-length emphasis stays a prompt
  instruction — a hard check that can fail four attempts in a row buys a silent morning, which
  is worse than a flat reading.
- Config: `astro2.accent_min` (4), `astro2.heavy_blocks` (3), `astro2.weight_max` (5, the top
  of his scale, used to say «4 з 5» rather than hardcoding it).
- Out of scope: the `pick` stage (accented blocks are already mandatory through
  `mandatory_ids`), and v1.
