# t1150 — the test fixture repeated a real day and one natal position

## Problem / goal

`tests/data/reference-day.txt` was committed (5551119) as "a synthetic day in his layout with
invented values". Re-read before the push, it is not:

- its first four lines are a translation of the reference astrologer's **real** page for that
  date — same hour, same Moon degree, same house, same meaning;
- its natal hit implies a natal Mercury within one degree of the owner's real one, which this
  repository deliberately keeps out of git (t1053: birth data never committed, the written
  natal portrait git-ignored).

Nothing was published — the finding came from the pre-push review, and the commit is still
local. The commit-time review of 5551119 asserted the opposite, which is the second half of
the problem: a review that states "invented" without checking is worse than no review.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** no line of any fixture under `tests/data/` appears verbatim in the real reference
  store (`status/state/astro-reference.jsonl`). Skipped when the store is absent, which is the
  state of a fresh checkout.
- **TC2 —** the fixture carries no position of his: the natal hit moves off Mercury, and the
  two house placements are shifted so the public sky plus a house number no longer discloses
  one of his cusps. (The real leak was the pair `(♅☍☿)` plus a degree.)
- **TC3 —** the phase-1 suite still passes unchanged against the edited fixture: the parser
  contract, the fidelity check and the mandatory-pick rules are what the fixture exists for.

## Approach

Edit the fixture so nothing in it is his: the frame line takes the degree the window formula
already implies (2°, not 13°), the house becomes 6 with its own meaning, and the natal hit
moves to Venus with a square — a pairing his chart does not carry. The two transit houses
move off his own (Neptune 1→2, Uranus 3→4): the degrees in the formula are the real sky of
that date, so the house number beside them was giving away where his cusps fall. The `ITEMS`
table in `tests/test_mood2_phase1.py` follows.

Out of scope: rewriting the published history. The leak never reached the remote.
