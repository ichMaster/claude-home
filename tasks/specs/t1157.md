# t1157 — the reading breathes

## Problem / goal

The owner, 2026-11-12: «додай більше пауз, бо це зараз тараторить в один суцільний текст і я
не встигаю усвідомити, що говориться». The v2 reading is around two thousand characters of
dense meaning delivered at Зоря's forecast tempo of 1.1 — one block, no silence between the
parts of the day, and nothing to think against.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** `voice_text()` puts `[довга пауза]` before each part of the day and announces the
  part before a `[пауза]` and its content.
- **TC2 —** the spoken copy carries no headings, no `ОСНОВА`, no `[pN]` markers.
- **TC3 —** the written message carries no tags: `[пауза]` is for the ear only.
- **TC4 —** the reading is delivered with the `zorya-reading` profile (tempo 0.95), not the
  forecast one.
- **TC5 —** `mood.send(voice=…)` uses the copy it was given rather than re-writing tags over
  it, and `mood.send(profile=…)` overrides the role's own profile.

## Approach

`voice_text()` in `status/mood2.py` builds the spoken copy deterministically: the part of the
day is known exactly, so a model call to guess where the breaks go would be both slower and
worse. `mood.send()` grows two optional arguments — `voice` (a prepared spoken copy) and
`profile` (the delivery) — and v2 passes both. A new `zorya-reading` profile in
`config.local.json` is the forecast voice at tempo 0.95 and steadier stability.
