# t1141 — weather every three hours, and the day's forecast in the morning

## Problem / goal

The owner asked (2026-11-09, by voice): «я хочу, щоби погоду ти присилав кожні три години. І
на початку дня, щоб ти робив мені прогноз погоди». Today the weather is only a line inside the
digest and the watcher, and there is no forecast for the day at all — the `weather` probe
reports the current state and today's min/max, nothing about when it rains or how the day moves.

Two things are needed:

1. **A day forecast**, sent once in the morning: how the temperature moves through morning,
   afternoon and evening, when precipitation falls, the wind, sunrise and sunset. Built for the
   ear — no Latin script, no abbreviations, units spelled out in Ukrainian words.
2. **The current weather every three hours** while he is awake, as its own message rather than
   buried in a digest.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** given the real wttr.in `format=j1` response for Lviv, `summarize()` returns a probe
  record whose text names all three parts of the day with temperatures, the wind, and sunrise
  and sunset in 24-hour form, and contains no Latin letters (the voice reads it).
- **TC2 —** given hourly slots with `chanceofrain` at 80% from 15:00 to 21:00, the text names
  that window and the severity is `warn`; given a dry day, the text says there are no
  precipitations and the severity is `info`.
- **TC3 — edge:** given a response whose `hourly` list only covers the night (slots 0 and 300),
  the probe still succeeds and names no part of the day it has no data for.
- **TC4 — edge:** given a gust above the warning threshold, or a minimum below the cold
  threshold, the severity is `warn` even on a dry day.

## Approach

A new probe `status/probes/forecast.py` with a pure `summarize(data, now=None)` so it is
testable offline; the fetch lives in `main()`. Thresholds and the TTL go to `config.json`
(`probes.forecast`); the city is shared with `probes.weather`.

`status/weather-send.sh` sends either one: no argument — the `weather` probe (current),
`--day` — the `forecast` probe. Two timers: `noos-weather.timer` at 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00
and 23:00, and `noos-weather-day.timer` at 08:00, which together give a message every three
hours from eight in the morning with the forecast first. Nothing fires inside the sleep window.

Out of scope: tomorrow's forecast, severe-weather alerts from a second source, and touching the
weather line in the digest.
