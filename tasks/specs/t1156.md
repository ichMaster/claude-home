# t1156 — nothing of his day may disappear

## Problem / goal

The owner, 2026-11-11, after the reading named a body the check then rejected:

> «Я тобі чітко вказав, яка причина для кожного тексту. Там було написано: факт — яка планета,
> в якому домі, в якому градусі, в якому знаці. Тобі треба взяти цей факт. Потім взяти те, що
> написано російською, перекласти українською. Потім зв'язати це пояснення з фактом
> літературно. А потім зробити саморевізію так, щоб не зникло згадування про планети. І плюс
> це погрупувати по часу доби. Які ти планети ставиш туди? Всі планети я тобі надав.»

The defect is mine and it is structural: `pick` narrowed the day to at most five positions
before the writing stage ever saw it, so planets he supplied were gone by the time the text
was written — and then the check complained about a body being "not in the day" when the day
did carry it and the selection did not.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** the writing stage receives **every** position of the day, not a selection.
- **TC2 —** a reading that drops a body the day carries fails the check, naming the body.
- **TC3 —** a reading that names a body the day does not carry still fails (t1155, unchanged).
- **TC4 —** the failure is fed back to the retry, and a corrected second attempt passes.
- **TC5 —** positions are grouped by part of day: a reading whose ПРОГНОЗ lines are not in
  `вночі → зранку → в обід → після обіду → ввечері → фоном` order fails.
- **TC6 —** the per-line and whole-reading limits follow the day's size rather than a fixed
  five positions.

## Approach

`run()` stops filtering the items by the selection: the writing stage gets all of them,
labelled with `коли` (t1153). `pick` stays — its answer becomes emphasis and order advice,
not a filter. `check_reading()` gains the missing-body rule beside the invented-body one, and
a part-of-day ordering rule. The prompt is rewritten around his four steps: take the fact,
translate his explanation, tie the two together in Ukrainian, group by part of day — and lose
nothing.
