# t1153 — the reading names parts of the day, not clock times

## Problem / goal

The owner, 2026-11-11 by voice: «не читалася година з такої-то до такої години, мені не
важливо з такої-то години — я би хотів, щоби було вночі, зранку, в обід, після обіду,
ввечері».

v1 was built the other way round: a clock window was what made a claim checkable in the
evening. That reasoning stays true and stays wrong for him — he listens, and a reading full
of `05:49` is a timetable, not a forecast. The exact hours do not disappear; they move to
`ОСНОВА`, which the voice never reads.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** `part_of_day("09:00", "11:00")` → `зранку`; `("21:27", "23:27")` → `ввечері`;
  `("01:00", "03:00")` → `вночі`; `("12:10", "13:40")` → `в обід`; `("14:30", "17:00")` →
  `після обіду`.
- **TC2 —** a window spanning parts takes the one it spends most of itself in:
  `("05:49", "11:34")` → `зранку` (five minutes of night do not make it a night window).
- **TC3 — edge:** a moment rather than a window (`to` is None) takes the part of its start;
  a position with no time at all returns an empty label, and the line is written without one.
- **TC4 — edge:** midnight is handled as night, and a window ending exactly on a boundary
  does not borrow the next part.
- **TC5 —** the writing stage receives the label: each selected position carries `коли` when
  it has a time.
- **TC6 —** `check_reading` rejects a clock time in the spoken parts (`ДЕНЬ`, `ПРОГНОЗ`,
  `ДІЯ`) and still allows it in `ОСНОВА`.

## Approach

`part_of_day()` in `status/mood2.py`, arithmetic: night 00–04, morning 05–11, midday 12–13,
afternoon 14–17, evening 18–23, resolved by which part the window overlaps most. `run()`
adds `коли` to each selected position before the writing stage, so the model reads a label
rather than inventing one. `day.md` says the windows are those words; `check_reading` grows
one rule — `\d{1,2}:\d{2}` anywhere the voice reads is a defect.

Out of scope: v1 and the evening question, both stopped (t1151).
