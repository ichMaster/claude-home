# t1232 — the t1224 duration guard drops genuine deadlines

## Problem / goal

c6d9ac5 (t1224) added duration words to the decimal guard, but the guard is applied
after *every* dotted candidate — including ones that cannot be decimals — and some of
the new stems match ordinary words. Reproduced at HEAD (all parsed correctly at
c6d9ac5^):

- «здати звіт до 20.12.2026 року» → no date («року» matches `рок\w*`, although a
  candidate with an explicit 4-digit year can never be a decimal);
- «замовити торт на 08.03 день народження» → no date (bare «день» in the list, though
  «день» never follows a decimal number grammatically);
- «полагодити до 15.04 хвіртку» → no date (`хв\w*` matches «хвіртку»);
- «відпустка 20.12 тиждень» → no date (`тиж\w*` matches «тиждень», which also never
  follows a decimal).

The deadline is silently dropped; the task lands undated in the backlog, so reminders
and the digest never fire for it.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** the four inputs above parse their dates again, with the
  duration phrase kept out of the cut (title keeps «день народження» etc.).
- **TC2 —** every t1224 case stays a number: «1.5 дня», «2.5 тижні», «1.5 хв»,
  «2.5 год», «1.5 місяця», «1.5 року», «2.5 хвилини» — no date, title unchanged.
- **TC3 — edge:** «до 20.12.2026 тис» (explicit year before a money word) still parses
  the date — the year exemption outranks every measure word.

## Approach

Two narrow changes to the guard at `tasks/store.py:135`:
1. skip the measure-word guard entirely when `m.group(3)` matched — an explicit year
   means the candidate cannot be a decimal;
2. tighten the stems that collide with ordinary words: bare «день» dropped (`дн\w*`
   already covers every genitive decimal form), `тиж\w*` → `тижн\w*` (covers
   тижня/тижні/тижнів, excludes «тиждень»), `хв\w*` → `хв\b|хвилин\w*` (covers the
   abbreviation and хвилина/хвилини/хвилин, excludes «хвіртку»).

The Latin/Cyrillic version guard is untouched (its word-boundary gap is a separate Low,
reported not fixed).
