# t1149 — a date and a time on every Telegram message

## Problem / goal

The owner asked by voice on 2026-11-11: every message must carry its date and time, and the
time must be *spoken* as "the twelfth of November, eleven o'clock five minutes" — day and
month, never the year.

He listens to Telegram rather than reading it, often hours after a message arrived, and a
forecast, a probe warning and a digest all sound alike with no moment attached. Telegram's own
timestamp is on screen, not in the audio.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** `stamp(dt)` for 2026-11-12 11:05 returns the written form `12 листопада, 11:05`
  and the spoken form `Дванадцяте листопада, одинадцята година п'ять хвилин.`
- **TC2 —** the spoken form never contains a Latin letter or a digit (it is read aloud).
- **TC3 —** no year appears in either form.
- **TC4 — edge:** a whole hour (11:00) speaks as `одинадцята година рівно`, with no minutes.
- **TC5 — edge:** minute agreement is Ukrainian, not English: 1 → `одна хвилина`,
  2 → `дві хвилини`, 5 → `п'ять хвилин`, 21 → `двадцять одна хвилина`,
  22 → `двадцять дві хвилини`.
- **TC6 — edge:** midnight is never a zero: 00:30 speaks as `дванадцята година тридцять хвилин ночі`, and 00:00 as `опівночі`.
- **TC7 —** every day of the month and every minute of the day produce a stamp with no digit
  in the spoken form (a sweep, so no hole in the tables goes unnoticed).
- **TC8 —** `notify.sh` prefixes the written stamp to the text part and the spoken stamp to
  the synthesized part, for the default, `--voice-only`, `--voice-text` and `--no-voice`.
- **TC9 —** `--no-stamp` sends exactly what was given, unchanged.
- **TC10 — edge:** a message long enough to be split carries the stamp on the first part only,
  not on every part.

## Approach

`status/tools/timestamp.py` — a pure function `stamp(dt)` returning `(written, spoken)`, with
Ukrainian ordinal and cardinal tables for the day, the hour and the minute. Hours are feminine
ordinals the way they are actually said (`одинадцята година`), midnight is `дванадцята година
ночі`.

`notify.sh` calls it once per run and prepends: the written stamp plus a blank line to the
text, the spoken stamp plus a newline to what is synthesized. One choke point, so timers,
digests, probes, restart notices and conversation all get it without touching each sender.

Out of scope: the `reply` tool path (text and files only — spoken answers already go through
`notify.sh`), and `status/bots/inbox.py`, which answers through `notify.sh` anyway.
