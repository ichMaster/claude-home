# t1175 — cal probe honors RECURRENCE-ID overrides of recurring events

## Problem / goal

`status/probes/cal.py` never reads RECURRENCE-ID or UID. When one occurrence of a recurring
Google Calendar event is moved (say from 10:00 to 15:00), the ICS carries the master VEVENT
with its RRULE intact plus an override VEVENT with RECURRENCE-ID and the new DTSTART. The
parser expands the master (still yielding 10:00) *and* lists the override as an independent
event (15:00) — so /calendar, the digest and the morning glance show a phantom slot that no
longer exists beside the real one.

Found by the nightly code review of 2026-11-13 (Medium, confirmed adversarially). No test
covered cal.py at all.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** a weekly event with one moved occurrence shows exactly the moved time in that
  week's window, not both (regression — two events before the fix).
- **TC2 —** an untouched week still expands from the master at the original time.
- **TC3 —** EXDATE-cancelled occurrences stay dropped (pins existing behaviour).

## Approach

Parse VEVENT blocks in two passes: first collect `(UID, RECURRENCE-ID)` pairs of override
blocks, then skip exactly those occurrences while expanding the master's RRULE. The override
block itself is parsed as before, so the moved instance appears once, at its new time. The
script tail moves under `main()` with a `__main__` guard so the parser is importable by
tests; behaviour as a probe is unchanged. Multiple EXDATE lines per VEVENT (Low finding)
stay out of scope.
