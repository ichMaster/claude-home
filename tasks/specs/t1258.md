# t1258 — store.py: a clock time written with a dot («о 9.05») is not a date

## Problem / goal

Verified by running the parser: `parse_due("подзвонити лікарю о 9.05")` returns due
2027-05-09 with title «подзвонити лікарю о», and «зустріч о 10.12 з Олегом» returns
2026-12-10 with title «зустріч о з Олегом». The t1215/t1232 preceding-word guard lists
Latin words, version words and measure words, but not the time prepositions «о/об» —
so any dot-written clock time whose minutes land in 1–12 becomes a real calendar date,
usually pushed to next year by the past-date rule. The task then sorts under a deadline
months away, the all-day mirror books the wrong day, and the time is cut out of the
title — the exact «confident wrong deadline costs more than an unparsed one» class
(t1048). Reachable from the /task Telegram flow and defer.

Minutes ≥ 13 already fall out via ValueError (no month 30); only 1–12 were the hole.
«з/до» stay out of the guard deliberately: «до 20.10» is the normal way to date a task.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** «подзвонити лікарю о 9.05» parses no date and keeps the title whole
  (regression: 2027-05-09 and a mutilated title before the fix).
- **TC2 —** «зустріч об 11.10 з Олегом» parses no date — «об» is the same preposition.
- **TC3 — edge:** «зустріч 20.12» still parses (a generic word before a date is the
  normal way to date a task), and a word merely *ending* in «о» («кіно 20.12») does
  not trip the guard.

## Approach

Add `\bоб?` to the preceding-word guard regex — word-bounded so only the standalone
preposition matches, never a word ending in «о». Smallest possible change, same shape
as the t1225 addition of the Cyrillic version words.
