# t1193 — АКЦЕНТ: the loud positions get a section of their own

## Problem / goal

The owner, 16 November: «перевірку залиши, але додай ще розділ Акцент і там підсвіти все що 3+
рангу».

So the reading gains a fifth part. Everything he marked three or more — `astro2.loud_min`,
which is already what `pressure()` calls `loud` — is named there in its own right, instead of
being left to compete for room inside a part-of-the-day line.

It also fixes what t1192 got wrong. That task made the accented window take «the longest
`ПРОГНОЗ` line of all», and the dry run of a real day shows the cost: two of four attempts
blew the 420-character line cap trying to obey it («рядок прогнозу довший за 420 знаків»). The
emphasis belongs in a section of its own, not in a line that has a listening budget. So the
longest-line rule goes and АКЦЕНТ replaces it.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** a reading with no `АКЦЕНТ` heading is caught as a missing part; the order check
  puts `АКЦЕНТ` after `ПРОГНОЗ` and before `ДІЯ`.
- **TC2 — regression:** a loud block (weight ≥ 3) that appears nowhere in `АКЦЕНТ` is reported;
  the same reading with it there passes.
- **TC3 —** `forecast_lines()` stops at `АКЦЕНТ`, so an accent line is not measured against the
  `ПРОГНОЗ` line cap and does not count as a part of the day for the ordering check.
- **TC4 —** a day with nothing loud demands no id in `АКЦЕНТ`: the section is still there and
  says the day is level.
- **TC5 —** `voice_text()` speaks the accent lines with their pause, and the heading itself is
  never read.
- **TC6 —** the writing stage is handed the loud ids as `{{ГОЛОСНІ}}`.

## Approach

- `SECTIONS` gains `АКЦЕНТ` between `ПРОГНОЗ` and `ДІЯ`; `accent_part()` cuts it out the way
  `summary_part()` cuts `ДЕНЬ`, and `forecast_lines()` stops there.
- `check_reading` requires every loud block to be marked in `АКЦЕНТ` — coverage only, so a
  quiet position mentioned beside one is a matter for the prompt and not a rewrite.
- `day.md` gains the section, the `{{ГОЛОСНІ}}` list, and the instruction that an accent line
  is the state unfolded rather than the `ПРОГНОЗ` line repeated. The t1192 longest-line rule
  is removed.
- Out of scope: the monthly and yearly forecasts he asked for in the same conversation — that
  is its own task.
