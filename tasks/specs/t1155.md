# t1155 — the cause is woven into the sentence

## Problem / goal

The owner, 2026-11-11: «оцю основу або факти якось гармонійно вписати в текст, який
говориться. Не зачитувати координати градусів, а літературно: хочеться відпочити і побути з
родиною, поїхати на природу — це тому, що Місяць зараз у Раку».

Two rules collided. ОСНОВА was removed from the chat (t1154) because a list of degrees is
not a forecast, and the writing prompt forbade naming a body or a sign in the spoken parts at
all — so what was left said *what* the day feels like and never *why*. He wants the why, in
plain speech: the planet and where it stands, as the reason for a state, inside the sentence.

What stays forbidden is unchanged: degrees, formulas, weights, clock times, textbook theory.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** a reading whose lines name a body present in the selection passes the check.
- **TC2 —** a body that is **not** in the day's selection is caught as invented
  (`Венера` named when the day carries only Moon, Mars and Jupiter).
- **TC3 —** inflected forms count: `Місяця`, `Місяцем`, `Венери`, `Марсом` resolve to the
  same body as the nominative.
- **TC4 —** «Луна» in a meaning is the same body as «Місяць» — his pages are Russian and the
  read stage translates, so both spellings must satisfy the same check.
- **TC5 —** a degree, a formula symbol, a weight mark or a clock time in the spoken part is
  still a defect (the t1153/t1143 rules are untouched).
- **TC6 —** a forecast line may now run to 200 characters, because it carries a cause.

## Approach

`bodies_named()` finds planet stems in the spoken part; `check_reading()` requires each one to
appear in the selection's `bodies` or `natal` fields, with `Луна` folded onto `Місяць`. The
prompt asks for the cause in half a sentence, in the words a person uses, and keeps the sign
or house taken from the selection rather than invented.

Signs are deliberately **not** checked in code: Ukrainian inflection would make the guard
either leaky or a source of false alarms that silence a morning. The prompt carries that rule
and the body check catches the case that matters.
