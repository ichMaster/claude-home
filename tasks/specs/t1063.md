# t1063 — panel: findings() counts sections after Low as findings

Fix from the phase-2 review (`status/state/code-review/2026-10-31-panel-phase2-scan.md`, M1).

## Problem / goal

`panel/changes.py::findings()` ends a severity section at the next *severity* heading or
EOF. Any other `## ` section after `## Low` — and the real phase-1 scan already carries
`## Checked and cleared` — donates its `### ` headings to the Low count. Those cleared
non-findings then enter `open_lows()` and sit in the standing Low list forever, since Lows
are never fixed and nothing removes them.

## Test cases

- **TC1 (regression)** — a report with `## Low` holding one `### ` finding, followed by
  `## Checked and cleared` holding two `### ` entries, yields exactly one Low; the cleared
  entries appear nowhere in the result. Fails before the change.
- **TC2** — the existing counts on the canonical SCAN fixture are unchanged
  (high 1 / medium 2 / low 1), i.e. the suite stays green.

## Approach

A severity section ends at the next `## ` heading of *any* kind, not only the next
severity heading. Nothing else changes: unrecognised reports still return `{}`.
