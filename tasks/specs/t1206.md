# t1206 — the panel hides deliberately-unfixed Lows from the «Накопичені Low» backlog

## Problem / goal

`open_lows` in `panel/changes.py` drops a Low whose title substring-matches the
concatenation of every fixes report — but fixes reports also *name* the Lows they
deliberately did NOT fix, under a «left open» heading. Executed against the real reports:
5 of ~137 Low titles are suppressed while still open (among them `panel/server.py:561` and
`voice/el_stt.sh:14`, which 2026-11-13-fixes.md explicitly lists as left as-is). The screen
whose stated purpose is «Lows accumulate where nobody sees the pile grow» under-reports the
pile — the "screen states something false" class the panel's rules forbid. Found by the
nightly review of 2026-11-18 (Medium).

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** a fixes report that names a Low only under a
  «## Left open deliberately» heading does NOT remove it from the backlog (fails before the
  change: the substring match suppresses it).
- **TC2 —** the same title named outside a left-open section still counts as fixed and is
  removed (the t1110 contract, kept — including a fixes file with no headings at all).
- **TC3 — edge:** the Ukrainian heading («Свідомо лишилось відкритим») is recognized too —
  one real report uses it.

## Approach

The text matched against is no longer the whole fixes file: sections whose `##` heading
says the findings were left open (English and Ukrainian phrasings seen in the real
reports: "left open", "не виправ", "відкрит") are cut out before matching. Exclusion, not
allowlisting of "Fixed" headings, because one real report (2026-11-14) titles its fixed
sections «High»/«Medium». A report with no headings keeps today's behaviour. Out of scope:
fuzzy title matching (the `key[:60]` substring rule itself stays as t1110 left it).
