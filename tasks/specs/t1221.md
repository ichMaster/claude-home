# t1221 — youtube probe honors the panel per-channel off switch

## Problem / goal

The panel's Sources screen writes `{"off": true}` into a channel's entry in
`config.local.json` (panel/sources_screen.py:156-158) and answers «вимкнено» — a documented
feature (docs/features/ADMIN-PANEL.md). But no consumer reads that key:
`status/probes/youtube.py:139` loads `probes.youtube.channels` and builds the polling
window (lines 184-191) with only PRIORITY and the rotating cursor as filters. A disabled
channel keeps being polled; its videos keep landing in `pending`, in the evening YouTube
digest, and in `youtube-archive.jsonl` that Bruno searches. The UI confirms a change that
has no effect anywhere, silently — a screen stating something false.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** given a channel list where one entry carries `"off": true`, when the probe
  selects what to poll, then that channel is excluded and the others stay (regression —
  fails before the change: the helper does not exist).
- **TC2 —** entries with `"off": false` or no `off` key are kept.
- **TC3 — edge:** `main()` actually routes the loaded list through the filter (wiring
  assertion on the source, same style as the shell-script tests).

## Approach

A small `active_channels(channels)` helper in `status/probes/youtube.py`, applied in
`main()` right after the not-configured check (so «канали не налаштовані» still means
not configured, not all-off). Priority entries obey the switch too — off means off.
