# t1072 — admin panel, phase 4: the Bruno screen

Phase 4 of `t1037` (`docs/features/ADMIN-PANEL.md`, screen 5).

## Problem / goal

The content channel decides twice and shows neither decision. It picks four news items out
of eight topics, and it drops candidates as repeats — by link, or by a 60% overlap of
headline words with something sent in the last fortnight. The dedup is the piece most
likely to be wrong, and today it is **completely invisible**: a story silently absent from
a digest looks exactly like a story that never existed.

The screen also carries the subscription archive, which answers the other recurring
question — "is that video even in there" — without a round trip through the bot.

## What has to be added before the screen can exist

`news_seen.py --filter` drops candidates and says only how many. The reason per candidate
is computed and thrown away. It now appends each drop to
`status/state/news-dropped.jsonl` — the headline, the link, the reason (`link` or
`overlap`), and for an overlap the earlier headline it matched and the score. Without that
record the screen's most valuable section would be empty.

## What it is

- **What was sent**: the news items from `news.json` and the last video digest, newest
  first, with the day each went out.
- **What was dropped as a repeat**: each dropped candidate with its reason and, for an
  overlap, the story it was judged a repeat of and the overlap score. This is the section
  the screen is for.
- **The subscription archive**: how many videos, the span of dates it really covers, and a
  box to look for a word in the titles. The box is a human looking something up, not the
  system judging relevance — that distinction is the owner's standing rule and the code
  says so where it would otherwise look like scoring.
- **Topics**: the eight files with their query, sources and limit.
- **Bruno's chat**: the last turns, how many answers in the current hour against the cap,
  and the dropped log with the shape of each dropped message.
- **The prompts** read-only, with a note that editing arrives in phase 5 where every
  prompt gets a diff and a restart banner.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — the drop record.** `news_seen.py --filter` writes one line per dropped candidate
  with reason `link` or `overlap`; an overlap line carries the matched headline and the
  score. Nothing is written when nothing is dropped.
- **TC2 — the record does not break the filter.** With an unwritable state directory the
  filter still filters and still prints its count; the record is best-effort.
- **TC3 — reading the drops.** `dropped_news()` returns them newest first, skips a torn
  line, and returns `[]` when the file is absent.
- **TC4 — the archive.** `archive_stats()` reports the count and the true first and last
  dates; `archive_search()` matches a word in a title case-insensitively and caps its
  results; both survive a torn line.
- **TC5 — the chat.** `bruno_chat()` reports the last turns, the number of answers inside
  the last hour against `telegram.inbox.max_per_hour`, and the dropped messages with their
  recorded shape.
- **TC6 — topics.** `topics()` reads the eight files, and a malformed one is reported as
  unreadable rather than taking the screen down.
- **TC7 — escaping and routing.** `/bruno` renders, and every headline, chat turn and
  topic query is escaped — news headlines and chat text are written by other people.

## Approach

`panel/bruno.py` for the collectors, pure over data passed in where it can be. The screen
is read-only: phase 4 adds no button. "Collect now" is in the design but it runs the news
collector against eight sources, which is an action, and actions on this screen can wait
until there is a reason to press one.
