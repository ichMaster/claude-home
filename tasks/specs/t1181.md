# t1181 — cal drops recurring all-day events with UNTIL

## Problem / goal

RFC 5545 requires UNTIL to match DTSTART's value type, so Google exports a recurring
all-day event as `DTSTART;VALUE=DATE:20261101` + `RRULE:FREQ=WEEKLY;UNTIL=20270101`.
`parse_dt` makes dtstart timezone-aware, and dateutil's `rrulestr` then raises
`ValueError: RRULE UNTIL values must be specified in UTC when DTSTART is timezone-aware`.
The `except` falls back to `starts=[start]` — the long-past first occurrence — so every
current occurrence silently disappears from /calendar, the digest and the watcher. The same
happens to a timed event whose UNTIL lacks the `Z` suffix.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** a weekly all-day event (`VALUE=DATE` DTSTART, date-form UNTIL in
  the future) is listed on its day inside the window (fails before: `events()` returns []).
- **TC2:** the UNTIL bound still holds — an occurrence after the UNTIL date is not listed.
- **TC3 — edge:** a timed event with a naive (no `Z`) datetime UNTIL expands instead of
  collapsing to the first occurrence.

## Approach

Normalise the rule before `rrulestr`: a date-form UNTIL becomes 23:59:59 local of that day
converted to UTC `…Z` (keeping the final day inclusive, as RFC intends for all-day); a naive
datetime UNTIL is read as local time and converted likewise. A `Z`-suffixed UNTIL passes
through untouched. The except fallback stays as the net for genuinely malformed rules.
