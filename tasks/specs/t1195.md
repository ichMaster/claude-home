# t1195 — his month and his year reach the day

## Problem / goal

The owner, 16 November: «ще треба зберігати місячні прогнози і річний прогноз і потім
додавати їх до денних прогнозів. Якщо в річних і місячний є конкретна дата то мерджити або
додавати її до дених прогнозів на момент генерації а якщо це період то сказати про нього в
кінці прогнозу в стилі на протязі з .. до .. буде ... і в такому стилі».

He forwarded both the same morning: the November month (8 dated events plus 8 «Весь ноябрь»
blocks) and the 2026 year (15 events, several carrying their own date range). They are stored
— `astro_learn.py add --period місяць|рік` — and nothing reads them. The daily reading is
built from his hourly calendar alone, so a day that his month calls one of the month's five
events sounds exactly like any other Tuesday.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** `astro_learn.covering(day)` returns the monthly reference whose stored date shares
  the day's year and month, and the yearly one whose date shares its year; nothing for a day
  in another month or year, and the latest record of each kind wins.
- **TC2 —** `wide_relevant(entries, day)` keeps a `date` entry only when its date *is* that
  day, and a `period` entry only when the day falls inside `[from, to]` — a period that ended
  yesterday and one that starts tomorrow are both dropped. Entries with an unreadable date are
  dropped and counted, never guessed.
- **TC3 —** a dated entry becomes a position of the day: it is appended to `items` with its own
  id, its weight counts towards `pressure()`, and a loud one is therefore mandatory and must be
  highlighted in `АКЦЕНТ` like any other.
- **TC4 —** the periods are capped at `astro2.wide_max` (3), chosen by weight, and what was
  dropped is reported through `note()` rather than silently vanishing.
- **TC5 —** the reading grows a `ПЕРІОД` part between `ДІЯ` and `ОСНОВА`; every selected period
  must be marked there, and a reading that omits one is reported.
- **TC6 —** `ПЕРІОД` carries no Arabic digit: the dates are spoken in words, because a figure
  comes back from the synthesizer in the wrong case. A day with no period at all still has the
  part, saying there is nothing holding.
- **TC7 —** a body that only the month or the year carries may be named without being called an
  invention, and a selected one that never sounds is still reported as dropped.
- **TC8 —** the extraction is cached by the hash of the two texts, so a month costs one call and
  not thirty; a changed text re-reads.

## Approach

- `astro_learn.covering(day)` — storage already holds the records, so this is a lookup.
- A new stage, `wide`, between `pick` and `day`: one call reads both texts into entries
  (`scope`, `kind`, `from`, `to`, `weight`, `bodies`, `meaning`, `natal`), and **Python decides
  what is relevant** — the model reads his prose, arithmetic answers «does this cover today».
  That is the standing division of labour.
- Dated entries join `items` with kind `wide` (counted by `blocks()`, so `pressure()` sees
  them); periods stay out of `items` and are passed to the writing stage and to
  `check_reading()` separately, so the month's background is never mistaken for the day's
  accent.
- The reading gains `ПЕРІОД`, the last thing spoken, in his own style: «протягом з … до … буде
  …», dates in words.
- Cached in `status/state/astro2/wide.json` by the hash of the two texts.
- Out of scope: generating a monthly or yearly reading of our own. These are his, read once a
  month, and only the day is written.
