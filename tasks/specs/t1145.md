# t1145 — astro v2, phase 3: choose the day's facts ourselves

> Outline. Phase 3 of `docs/astro-v2-reference.md`, and the point at which v2 stops needing his
> text at all. Sharpened when phase 2 clears its gate.

## Problem / goal

Our ephemeris computes a day in more detail than his calendar prints, and that is the problem:
v1 named the season and buried the Moon. Phase 3 is the ranking — his foreground, our figures.

Three things his day is made of that `status/astro.py` does not produce yet:

- **the ingress moment** — when the Moon changes sign; a boundary solve on the sign cusp;
- **the void-of-course window** — from the Moon's last aspect to that ingress, which in his
  calendar carries a meaning of its own — the hurry to finish under the old programme before
  the new sign starts one from scratch;
- **an intensity tier** — his one to five `✤`. We score `1/(orb + 0.5)` internally for the
  day's focus and never surface a tier; a published tier is what gives a day a shape instead
  of a flat list.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** the ingress solver returns the Moon's sign change to the nearest minute, verified
  against two dates computed independently (a second ephemeris call bracketing the boundary);
  a day with no ingress returns none rather than a false one at midnight.
- **TC2 —** the void window starts at the Moon's last aspect before the ingress and ends at the
  ingress; a day with no ingress has no void window.
- **TC3 —** the intensity tier is a pure function of computed quantities (orb, bodies, whether
  the target is natal) and is stable: the same day scored twice gives the same tiers.
- **TC4 —** selection overlap against a held-back day of his calendar is computed in both
  directions — of his windows, how many we named in the same tier; of ours, how many he
  ignored — and stored per day.
- **TC5 —** every factor he named that we failed to compute is reported as a gap in the
  ephemeris, distinct from one we computed and ranked low; the two failures need different
  fixes.
- **TC6 — edge:** a day where the Moon makes no aspect at all still produces a ranked list
  from sign, house and phase rather than an empty one.

## Approach

Arithmetic first — the ingress and the void window are cheap and they are exactly what his
foreground is built from. The tier after that, calibrated so our tiers land where his do on the
days already on file. The ranking prompt last, since it is the only part that needs judgment.

Deliberately out of scope: the switch itself (step 4 of the roadmap), which waits on the
owner's verdict after v1 and v2 have been heard side by side.
