# t1110 — the Changes screen told three untruths

Found by the full review, 2026-11-01 (truth H1, H3 and M8 in
`status/state/code-review/2026-11-01-panel-full-truth.md`).

## Problem

This screen exists to make the development process visible. All three of its claims about
today's work were wrong.

- **H1 — «не записано» about readings that exist.** The lookup used the full 40-character
  sha; 25 of the 39 readings on disk are named with the 7-character one git prints, including
  **every** panel commit of today. `CLAUDE.md` says `commit-review/<sha>.md` without saying
  which spelling, so both writers were within the letter of it. This is the one claim on the
  panel a reader acts on — the morning push is conditional on the reading existing.
- **H3 — zero findings for a night that found thirteen.** `findings()` matched only `###`
  headings; the nightly review writes numbered list items with bold titles. It therefore
  returned `{severity: []}` — truthy, so `parsed` was True and the counts rendered as real
  zeros, and the eight Lows never reached «Накопичені Low». Two of that night's Mediums were
  real enough to be fixed the same morning.
- **M8 — the Low backlog listed Lows that were already fixed**, which is the opposite of
  "the honest backlog of known-but-ignored".

## Fix

- The reading is looked up under both spellings.
- `findings()` reads numbered and bulleted items as well as `###` headings, with the bold
  title allowed to wrap across lines (it regularly does).
- `verdict_counts()` reads the report's own verdict line and **wins** where it exists: it is
  the author's own count, and a parser that does not know a shape should not outvote it.
  `parsed` is false when neither the verdict nor any finding was understood, so an
  unrecognised report shows «не розібрано» instead of zeros.
- `open_lows()` drops a Low whose title appears in any fix report.

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** a numbered nightly report yields its findings by severity. (Fails before: zero.)
- **TC2 —** a report in neither shape is not counted as clean: `findings` is `{}`,
  `verdict_counts` is `{}`, and the night renders «не розібрано».
- **TC3 —** the verdict line is read, and the night's counts match it.
- **TC4 —** the Lows of a numbered report reach the backlog.
- **TC5 —** a Low named in a fix report is not listed as open.
- **TC6 —** the reading is found under the short sha, still found under the long one, and a
  commit with no reading still says so. (TC6a fails before.)
