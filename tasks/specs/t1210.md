# t1210 — for_day returns a month or year forward as the day's calendar

## Problem / goal

A month or year forward is stored under an arbitrary day of its span (`add --period
місяць --day 2026-12-01`, the t1198 contract), but `for_day()` filters references by
`day` only, never by `period`, and returns `refs[-1]["text"]`. On the day such a forward
is stored under, the 08:15 v2 run (`mood2.reference_text`) receives the whole month's
text as that day's hourly calendar: with no daily calendar the run builds and sends a
day reading out of a month's windows instead of stopping with NoCalendar; with a daily
calendar present but the month appended later, the month silently replaces it, and
`check_reading` validates against the wrong source. The live store already holds a
місяць record under 2026-11-01 appended after that day's daily record. v1's
`mood.reference_block` cross-checks against the same wrong text. Found by the nightly
self-review of 2026-11-19 (Medium).

## Test cases

- **TC1 —** given a daily record and a later `місяць` record stored under the same day,
  when `for_day(day)` is called, then it returns the daily text, not the month's.
  (regression — fails before the change)
- **TC2 —** given a day carrying only a `місяць` record, `for_day(day)` returns "" — so
  the v2 run stops with its missing-calendar notice instead of reading a month as a day.
- **TC3 — edge:** a `рік` record behaves the same; records with no `period` field (old
  lines) still count as daily.

## Approach

`for_day()` keeps only references whose period is daily (`день`, or absent/empty for old
lines) — `тиждень` records are stored under their real days but are not hourly calendars
either and were never routed here by the add contract; they are left out of scope since
`references(day)` matching them requires an exact-day match that the weekly flow does not
use. `covering()` is untouched: months and years keep reaching the day through `wide`.
