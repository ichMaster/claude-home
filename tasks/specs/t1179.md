# t1179 — check_reading whitelists bodies from everyday words in the day's prose

## Problem / goal

`check_reading` builds `available` — the set of bodies the day carries — with the **wide**
matcher over `bodies`, `natal` **and** `meaning`. `meaning` is his interpretive prose, where
«вузол» is an everyday noun; the loose branch reads it as the lunar nodes. Two failures,
both demonstrated:

- a nodeless day whose item meaning says «Вузол напруги у розмові сам не розв'яжеться»
  makes the lost check refuse a correct nodeless reading with «світила доби, яких у читанні
  немає: вузли» — the exact t1177 failure class, reproduced from the items side, burning
  retries or killing the 08:15 send;
- if the rewrite complies with «місячні вузли», the invented check passes, because
  `invented = bodies_claimed(head) - available` subtracts the wide set — a body asserted
  over a day that does not carry it goes unflagged.

The t1177 asymmetry («ambiguous evidence may acquit, never convict») was applied only to the
reading's words, not to the day's.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** items with no node positions but an everyday «вузол» in one
  `meaning`; a correct nodeless reading passes `check_reading` with no «яких у читанні
  немає: вузли» complaint (fails before the change).
- **TC2:** same items; a reading asserting «місячні вузли» is flagged «світила, яких у добі
  немає: вузли» (fails before the change — it currently passes clean).
- **TC3 — edge:** a real node day — bare «вузли» in an item's **bodies** field — still
  whitelists the nodes (a reading naming «місячні вузли» passes) and still demands them
  (a reading silent on them is flagged lost): no regression of t1156/t1159.

## Approach

Match by what the field is, not one way for everything: `bodies` and `natal` are structured
body-name fields, where a bare «вузли» can only be the nodes — they stay **wide**; `meaning`
is prose, where only unambiguous words count — it goes **strict**. One `available` set,
`bodies_named(structured) | bodies_claimed(prose)`, feeds both the invented and the lost
checks. Out of scope: the Low finding about missing declensions (-ові, «Ліліттю»), reported
separately.
