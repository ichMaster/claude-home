# t1194 — his compact body notation hides a body from the fidelity check

## Problem / goal

Measured on a real day (15 November, dry run of 2026-11-16): the reading failed all four
attempts and the morning would have been silent.

```
читання не пройшло перевірку (1): світила, яких у добі немає: Сонце
читання не пройшло перевірку (2): світила, яких у добі немає: вузли
читання не пройшло перевірку (3): світила, яких у добі немає: Сонце
```

The Sun *was* in the day, three times over: his multi-day entries name the pair in one token
— `"bodies": "Солнце90Марс"`, `"bodies": "Солнце90Раху"`, `"bodies": "Солнце90Кету"` — his
shorthand for «Sun square Mars at 90°». `bodies_named()` matches each body with a word
boundary at both ends (t1177), and in `Солнце90Марс` there is no boundary between `е` and
`9`: both are word characters. So the day's own Sun, Rahu and Ketu are invisible to
`available`, and the check then
accuses a correct reading of inventing them — while a reading that quietly drops them is
never caught, which is the t1156 failure the check exists to prevent.

It passed yesterday only by luck: the model happened not to name the Sun.

## Test cases

- **TC1 — regression:** a day carrying `"bodies": "Солнце90Раху"` and a reading that names the
  Sun and the nodes → `check_reading` reports no invented body. Fails before the change
  («світила, яких у добі немає: Сонце, вузли»), passes after.
- **TC2 —** the same day with a reading that never mentions the Sun → it is reported as a body
  of the day that never sounded. The check has to work in both directions, or the fix trades
  one blindness for another.
- **TC3 —** `bodies_named("Солнце90Марс")` resolves to both the Sun and Mars.
- **TC4 — edge:** the digits are his angle shorthand and nothing else: `bodies_named` on a
  spaced, ordinary field («Марс і Кету») is unchanged, and a degree inside his prose
  (`meaning`) still matches strictly, so «вузол» in his Russian text convicts nobody (t1179).

## Approach

In `check_reading`, normalise the **structured** fields before matching: every run of digits
becomes a space, so `Солнце90Раху` reads as `Солнце Раху` and the anchored patterns match as
they were designed to. Only `bodies` and `natal` are normalised — they are name fields. His
`meaning` prose keeps its strict matching, degrees and all.

Out of scope: the reading's own text, which is Ukrainian prose and never carries his shorthand.
